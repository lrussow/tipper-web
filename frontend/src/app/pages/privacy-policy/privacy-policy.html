The Tipper™ ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a user of our mobile application and website (collectively, the "Service").

Information We Collect

Account Information

When you register, we collect your name, email address, and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth).

Payment & Banking Information

We do not store your bank account details or payment card numbers directly. All payment processing is handled by Stripe. When you connect a bank account or accept tips, Stripe collects and securely stores your financial information under their own privacy policy. We retain only a Stripe account identifier to associate your profile with your Stripe Express account.

Transaction Data

We store records of tip transactions associated with your account, including amounts, timestamps, and anonymised payer identifiers. This data is used to display your transaction history and calculate payouts.

Device & Usage Data

We may collect basic device information (OS version, app version) and usage logs for the purpose of diagnosing errors and improving the Service. Logs are retained for a maximum of 90 days.

How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account
  • To process tip payments via Stripe
  • To display your transaction history
  • To communicate important service updates and support responses
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse
  • To comply with applicable laws and Stripe's platform requirements

We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.

Stripe

Payment processing is provided by Stripe, Inc. By using The Tipper, you also agree to Stripe's Privacy Policy. Stripe is responsible for the security and handling of all payment and banking data you provide during onboarding and payout setup.

Data Sharing

We share your data only in the following circumstances:

  • Stripe — to process payments and manage your connected account
  • Supabase — our database and authentication provider
  • Legal obligations — when required by law or to protect the rights of users

Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal information will be removed within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for fraud prevention purposes. Transaction records may be retained for up to 7 years to comply with financial record-keeping requirements.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data
  • Withdraw consent at any time (where processing is consent-based)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at lrussow@gmail.com.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, Supabase Row-Level Security, and Stripe's PCI-compliant infrastructure to protect your data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
